A clean, professional personal brand block theme for thought leaders, coaches and executives.

== Description ==

Meridian is a block theme for personal brand websites — ideal for leadership coaches, executives, authors, speakers and consultants.

A sticky profile sidebar (logo, site title, tagline, short bio and social links) sits beside your content on wide screens and stacks above it on mobile. Ready-made patterns help you build an about or landing page in minutes:

* Hero Bio
* Expertise Grid
* Experience Timeline
* Talks & Speaking
* Publications List
* Volunteering & Causes
* Work With Me Call to Action

Templates included: Blog, Single Post, Page, Archive, Search and 404, plus three page templates you can pick per page: Landing Page (no title), No Sidebar and Full Width.

Fonts (Inter and DM Serif Display) are bundled with the theme, so no requests are made to third-party font servers.

== Installation ==

1. In your admin panel, go to Appearance > Themes and click Add New Theme.
2. Search for Meridian, then click Install and Activate.
3. Go to Appearance > Editor to customize your site.

== Frequently Asked Questions ==

= Does this theme require any plugins? =

No. Meridian works with WordPress core blocks and the Site Editor only.

= How do I build a profile homepage? =

Create a new page, choose the "Landing Page (no title)" template in the page settings, and insert patterns from the "Meridian: Hero & Bio" and "Meridian: Content" categories. Then go to Settings > Reading and set that page as your homepage.

= How do I edit the sidebar? =

Go to Appearance > Editor > Patterns > Template Parts > Sidebar. The logo, site title and tagline come from your site settings; the bio text and social links can be edited directly.

= How do I change the colors and fonts? =

Go to Appearance > Editor > Styles.

= Can I use a page without the sidebar? =

Yes. When editing a page or post, open the Template setting and choose "No Sidebar" or "Full Width".
